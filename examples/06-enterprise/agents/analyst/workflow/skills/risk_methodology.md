# Risk Assessment Methodology

## Framework
- **Likelihood Assessment**: Rate probability of adverse events (1-5 scale)
- **Impact Assessment**: Rate potential damage (1-5 scale)
- **Risk Score**: Combine as (likelihood * impact) / 25, normalized to 0.0-1.0
- **Risk Categories**: Low (0-0.3), Medium (0.3-0.6), High (0.6-0.8), Critical (0.8-1.0)

## Best Practices
- Always consider historical data when available
- Document assumptions explicitly
- Consider both quantitative and qualitative factors
- Flag any data quality issues that may affect the assessment
