# Risk Analysis Domain Knowledge

## Risk Categories
- **Operational Risk**: Risks from internal processes, people, and systems
- **Market Risk**: Risks from market movements and economic factors
- **Credit Risk**: Risks from counterparty defaults
- **Compliance Risk**: Risks from regulatory non-compliance

## Assessment Framework
- Use quantitative metrics where possible
- Consider both probability and impact
- Document all assumptions
- Cross-reference with historical data
